How often should I service my AC in Dubai?
The company recommends having your AC checked at least twice a year. The right schedule can depend on the system, usage and property conditions.
What are common signs that my AC needs repair?
Common signs include water leakage, unusual noise, weak airflow, higher electricity bills or a system that is no longer cooling as expected.
Do you provide emergency AC services?
The company states that it offers 24/7 emergency AC assistance. Call the published contact number to discuss your requirements.
Do you service homes and commercial properties?
Yes. The company lists services for apartments, villas, offices, shops and commercial properties.
Which AC systems do you work with?
The company lists split, central, package, ducted split, VRF, FCU, chilled-water, chiller, window, cassette and floor-standing systems.
Can I request a quote online?
Yes. Use the request-a-quote page to submit your contact details and service requirements.
